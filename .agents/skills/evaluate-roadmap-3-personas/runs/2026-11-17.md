This is a reassessment of **stored evidence**, not a new external-source audit. All 317 official-source dates and 340 third-party-source dates remain `2026-05-02`. The ordinary ordered tiers use the median of the three votes. The existing `median_tier` convention in `scripts/run_3_persona_eval.py` is retained: any `specialty` vote selects that unordered track. The seed script itself, its fixed judgments and its fallback heuristics were **not executed**. This inherited convention is exposed in the audit rather than silently resolved by changing individual judgments.

For the disagreement gate, specialty votes are excluded from tier distance. We also checked the remaining ordered pair in mixed-specialty records, stricter than the seed helper's whole-record exclusion. Both interpretations pass: **0 of 317** records exceed a one-tier distance, against the unchanged maximum share of 10%.
